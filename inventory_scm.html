dashboard_customize Inventory Management Overview

Inventory Management in Oracle Fusion SCM controls stock levels, tracks material movements, supports multiple inventory organizations, and enables accurate valuation and visibility across the supply chain.

It integrates tightly with Procurement, Manufacturing, Order Management, and Costing to ensure real-time stock accuracy and efficient material handling.

Used In
  • Receiving & Put-away
  • Cycle Counting & Physical Inventory
  • Material Transactions
Who Needs Inventory Mgmt?
  • Inventory Controllers
  • Warehouse Supervisors
  • Supply Chain Analysts
fact_check Main Inventory Principles
  • Multi-org Structure: Supports multiple inventory organizations with different controls
  • Subinventories & Locators: Granular storage control
  • Transaction Types: Receipts, issues, transfers, adjustments
  • ABC / Cycle Counting: Prioritized inventory accuracy checks
  • Min-Max & Replenishment: Automated stock level management
