Lecture Notes

In this lesson, we explore the Eisenhower Matrix, a simple decision-making tool that helps you make the distinction between tasks that are important, not important, urgent, and not urgent. The matrix consists of four quadrants that classify your daily tasks based on these two parameters, allowing you to prioritize effectively and reduce stress.

What you'll learn:

  • check How to categorize tasks into the 4 quadrants (Do, Decide, Delegate, Delete).
  • check Strategies for minimizing time spent in the "Urgent but Not Important" quadrant.
  • check Practical tips for scheduling "Important but Not Urgent" tasks for long-term growth.
