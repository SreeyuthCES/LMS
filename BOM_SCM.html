dashboard_customize Bill of Materials Overview

Bill of Materials (BOM) in Oracle Fusion SCM defines the hierarchical structure of components, subassemblies, and quantities needed to manufacture, assemble, or package a finished product.

BOM is the foundation for costing, material planning, work order execution, shop floor control, and variance analysis in manufacturing processes.

Used In
  • Work Orders & WIP
  • Material Requirements Planning
  • Standard Costing
Who Needs BOM?
  • Manufacturing Engineers
  • Cost Accountants
  • Supply Chain Planners
fact_check Main BOM Principles
  • Multi-level Structure: Supports nested assemblies and subassemblies
  • BOM Types: Standard, Model, Option Class, Common, Alternate
  • Effectivity Control: Date-effective, serial/lot effective, or revision-based
  • Phantom BOM: Components are exploded into parent during planning/execution
  • Cost Rollup: Automatically calculates standard cost from component costs
