dashboard_customize Manufacturing / Work in Process (WIP) Overview

Manufacturing / Work in Process (WIP) in Oracle Fusion SCM manages the execution of production on the shop floor, including work order release, material issue, operation completion, resource usage, and completion transactions.

It bridges planning, BOM, inventory, and costing to enable discrete, process, or repetitive manufacturing with real-time visibility and variance tracking.

Used In
  • Work Order Execution
  • Shop Floor Transactions
  • Cost & Variance Analysis
Who Needs WIP?
  • Shop Floor Operators
  • Production Supervisors
  • Manufacturing Engineers
fact_check Main WIP Principles
  • Work Order Driven: Discrete jobs or process batches
  • Operation-based: Sequential or non-linear routing steps
  • Material & Resource Tracking: Issue, return, charge at operation level
  • Completion & Scrap: Final assembly move-out, yield loss handling
  • Cost Collection: Captures material, resource, overhead variances
