About this lesson

Remote work requires a shift in how we communicate. Without the benefit of non-verbal cues and spontaneous desk chats, teams must be intentional about their interactions. In this lesson, we explore strategies to overcome the distance gap, including synchronous vs. asynchronous communication methods and establishing digital etiquette.

Key Takeaways

  • check Understanding the "Virtual Distance" phenomenon and its impact on trust.
  • check Best practices for video conferencing: When to turn the camera on.
  • check The golden rule of remote work: Over-communicate, but concisely.
