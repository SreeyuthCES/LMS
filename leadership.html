About this lesson

In this lesson, we explore the psychology of change management and how to keep morale high during organizational shifts. We will deep dive into the Kübler-Ross Change Curve and apply it to modern corporate restructuring scenarios.

Dr. Jenkins shares three critical strategies for communication during uncertain times: Transparency, Empathy, and Repetition. By the end of this video, you will be able to formulate a communication plan for your direct reports.
