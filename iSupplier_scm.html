dashboard_customize iSupplier Portal Overview

iSupplier Portal is the self-service supplier collaboration platform in Oracle Fusion SCM. It allows external suppliers to interact directly with the buying organization for purchase orders, invoices, payments, ASN, and performance visibility.

It reduces manual communication, speeds up processes, improves data accuracy, and strengthens supplier relationships in the procure-to-pay cycle.

Used In
  • PO Acknowledgment & ASN
  • Supplier Invoice Submission
  • Payment & Remittance Advice
Who Needs iSupplier?
  • Suppliers / Vendors
  • Procurement Teams
  • Accounts Payable Staff
fact_check Main iSupplier Principles
  • Self-Service: Suppliers manage their own data and transactions
  • Real-time Visibility: Suppliers see PO status, invoices, payments
  • Electronic Collaboration: ASN, invoices, change requests online
  • Secure Access: Role-based, supplier-specific login
  • Integration: Bi-directional sync with Fusion Procurement & Payables
