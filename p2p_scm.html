dashboard_customize Procurement / Purchasing (P2P) Overview

Procurement / Purchasing (Procure-to-Pay or P2P) in Oracle Fusion SCM manages the complete cycle from requisition creation to supplier payment, ensuring controlled spending, supplier collaboration, and accurate financial recording.

It integrates with Inventory, Payables, Projects, and Costing modules to provide end-to-end visibility and compliance in the source-to-pay process.

Used In
  • Requisitions & Purchase Orders
  • Supplier Invoicing & Payments
  • Contract & Supplier Management
Who Needs P2P?
  • Procurement Specialists
  • Buyers / Purchasing Agents
  • Accounts Payable Teams
fact_check Main P2P Principles
  • Requisition-to-Receipt: Controlled approval and conversion to PO
  • Three-way Match: PO – Receipt – Invoice matching for payment
  • Supplier Collaboration: Portal for quotes, invoices, and ASN
  • Blanket / Contract Purchase Agreements: For recurring purchases
  • Encumbrance Accounting: Budget reservation on commitment
