dashboard_customize Product Info Management Overview

Product Info Management (PIM) in Oracle Fusion SCM is the central hub for creating and maintaining all item master data across the enterprise.

It ensures consistent product information across Procurement, Inventory, Manufacturing, Order Management, and Planning modules.

Used In
  • Item Creation & Maintenance
  • Attribute Management
  • Global Item Master
Who Needs PIM?
  • SCM Functional Consultants
  • Master Data Team
  • Product Managers
fact_check Main PIM Principles
  • Single Source of Truth: One item record used everywhere
  • Attribute-driven: Flexible attributes for different item types
  • Change Management: Approval workflows for item changes
  • Multi-Org Access: Items visible across organizations
  • Integration Ready: Feeds data to all downstream modules
